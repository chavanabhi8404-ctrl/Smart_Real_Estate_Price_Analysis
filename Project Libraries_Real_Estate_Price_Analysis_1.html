Smart Real Estate Price Analysis.¶

Project Domain:- Estate Analytics| Data Analytics | Business Intelligence¶

Project Objective:-¶

-The objectives of this project is to analyze housing market data to identify key factors influencing house prices, clean real-world messy data containing missing values, and present visual insights that support data-driven decision making for real-estate businesses, buyers, and investors.

Skills¶

1.Python 2.Pandas 3.Numpy 4.Data Cleaning & Preprocessing 5.Missing Values Treatment 6.Exploratory Data Analysis(EDA) 7.Data Visualization (Matplotlib & Seaborn) 8.Business Insight Generation

Dataset Description:¶

  • Dataset Name: King Country House Sales Dataset (With missing values)
  • Records: 21,613
  • Features: 22
  • Target Variable: price

Key Attributes:¶

  • Property size (sqft_living, sqft_lot)
  • Structural details (bedrooms, bathrooms, floors)
  • Location features (zipcode, waterfront, view)
  • Renovation and cinstruction data

step 3: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)¶

import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
import seaborn as sns

Q1.What is the distribution of house prices?¶

In [11]:
plt.figure()
plt.hist(df['price'],bins=30)
plt.title("Distribution of Prices")
plt.xlabel("Price")
plt.ylabel("Frequency")
plt.show()

Analysis :-¶

  • Distribution is Right Skewed.
  • Majority houses are in the mid price range.

Conclusion:¶

  • Luxury houses are fewer, market is dominated by affordable properties.

Q2. How does living area affect price?¶

In [12]:
plt.scatter(df['sqft_living'], df['price'])
plt.xlabel("sqft of Living")
plt.ylabel("Price")
plt.title("Price vs Living Area")
plt.show()

Analysis :-¶

  • Strong positive correlation.

Conclusion:-¶

  • Bigger homes generally cost more.

Q3. What is the relationship between bedrooms and Price.¶

In [13]:
sns.boxplot(x = 'bedrooms', y='price', data=df)
plt.title("Bedrooms vs Price")
plt.show()

Analysis :-¶

  • Price increases till certain bedroom count.

Conclusion:-¶

  • After 6 bedrooms, price growth slows.

Q4. Do bathrooms impack house price?¶
